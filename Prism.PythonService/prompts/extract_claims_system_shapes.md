Four extractor rules, matching the system prompt's existing shapes:
(E1) Hedged/in-principle claims ('in principle', 'we suspect') are still claims about
this paper's own method or result — extract them; skip only when the hedge attaches
to field-level background.
(E2) A table title or caption is extractable only when it states a result or
comparison, not merely the axes being plotted; also extract any sentence quoting the
table's numbers.
(E3) When an overreaching claim appears beside a narrower sibling about one dataset,
setting or subset, extract both; never invent a broad claim the paper itself never
states.
(E5) A bare 'we observed X' sentence is a claim when X is a result about this paper's
own method, not setup or monitoring narration.
