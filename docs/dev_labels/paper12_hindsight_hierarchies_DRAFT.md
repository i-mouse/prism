## Rejected from AG (do not add as rows)
- Sec 2 "with the aim of improving the hierarchies available for further training": states an aim, not a result.
- Sec 4.2 nested test-time loop "may increase success probability": a design option and general knowledge.
- Sec 6 "the model might learn to ignore the proposed ideas": a hypothesized risk, not a claim.
- Sec 1 "intended to provide guidance that could plausibly be followed ...": design intent (prompt-level anti-hindsight-bias rules in App A.3).
- Sec 1 "The solver then repeatedly attempts the problem": the general definition allows N >= 1; App A.6 states N = 1 for the Lean instantiation and explains why, so it is carved out.
- Sec 3.1 Verify "terminates on every input": stated as an assumption; Sec 4 footnote 2 handles Lean non-termination with timeouts.
- Sec 2 contrast with Lean-STaR / DeepInsight: conceptual comparison of algorithms, not an empirical comparison.
